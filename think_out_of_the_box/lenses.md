# The moves

`think_out_of_the_box` uses these eight moves to step off the usual path. Each one is a question you ask the problem.
The examples are small and made up, to show the shape of the move, not to prove it works.

## 1. Invert it

**Ask:** How would I make this problem worse on purpose? What would I do to guarantee failure? Then flip each of those.
**Example:** A cafe wants more customers. To drive customers away: make it slow, crowded, with nowhere to sit. Flip: sell the opposite of crowded. **Idea:** a bookable quiet hour at 8 to 9 am, with a small fee that comes off the coffee.
**Trap:** flipping into the obvious opposite ("be less slow"). Flip into something you can sell or do.

## 2. Remove the biggest constraint

**Ask:** If the one thing that blocks me did not exist, what would I do? Then ask what that would really buy me, and get that another way.
**Example:** A freelancer cannot afford ads. With unlimited money they would buy attention. What does attention buy? Trust from strangers. **Idea:** do one free small job for three people whose word carries weight in the niche, and ask for a one line comment.
**Trap:** stopping at "if I had money". The value is in what the money would buy.

## 3. Add an absurd constraint

**Ask:** What if I could only do this in 7 minutes, with no screen, with no money, or with a stranger? What does that force?
**Example:** Studying for an exam. Absurd rule: study in 7 minute bursts, each in a different room, and never reread. **Idea:** question only rounds: write 5 questions on the topic, answer them from memory, check, move rooms.
**Trap:** an absurd rule that you ignore afterwards. Keep the rule until the idea appears.

## 4. Borrow from a distant field

**Ask:** Who has solved a problem with the same shape, in a field that looks unrelated (hospitals, airlines, farming, sport, theatre, trade routes, game design)? Take the mechanism, not the topic.
**Example:** A clinic waiting room is stressful. Airports solved a similar problem by showing progress. **Idea:** a board that shows "you are about 5th, roughly 25 minutes", and updates when it changes.
**Trap:** borrowing a buzzword ("gamify"). Name the exact mechanism and where it came from.

## 5. Solve a different problem

**Ask:** What is this really for? Is there a cheaper or easier way to reach that goal that skips this problem?
**Example:** "I need more followers." The real goal is to get hired. **Idea:** skip the audience and send a specific, useful note to 10 people who hire for that job.
**Trap:** changing the goal so far that the user's need disappears. Check the new goal still serves the old one.

## 6. Make the problem disappear

**Ask:** What would have to be true for this problem not to exist? What created it in the first place?
**Example:** Weekly status meetings run too long because information is trapped in people's heads. **Idea:** a shared page everyone updates before 10 am, and meetings only when a decision is needed.
**Trap:** "just don't do it" with no replacement. Say what carries the need that the old thing served.

## 7. Give it to the opposite person

**Ask:** Who is on the other side of this problem? What if they ran it, or did the job?
**Example:** Parents cannot get a teenager to study. **Idea:** the teenager teaches the parent one topic each week, and the parent plays the confused beginner.
**Trap:** an idea that only embarrasses the other person. It must give them something they want.

## 8. Subtract

**Ask:** What if I stopped doing something instead of adding something? What is the smallest version? What would I remove first?
**Example:** A team sends reports nobody replies to. **Idea:** stop sending them for a month and see who asks.
**Trap:** removing something that people rely on without telling them. Test on a small scale first.

---

## Fake out of the box (do not count these)

These sound fresh but fit almost any problem. If an idea is one of these, it belongs in the obvious list, or it must be sharpened until it is about this problem only.

- "Build a community around it."
- "Gamify it."
- "Partner with influencers."
- "Use AI (or blockchain) for it."
- "Make it a subscription."
- "Tell a better story."
- "Collect data first."
- "Do a survey."
