# Getting your first 10 paying customers

My assumptions, not facts: Hong Kong shops, you shoot on your phone, and a typical job takes about 3.5 hours (shoot, edit, deliver). That makes 10 customers about 35 hours of production. The rest of your time goes to selling, so plan on about 8 weeks at 10 hours a week.

## The offer

Don't sell "video production". Sell one fixed package:

> **"3 short clips for your shop, shot in one 1-hour visit, delivered in 5 days. HKD 800. One revision included."**

- **Price:** HKD 800 is a testing price, so adjust it. Raise it to about HKD 1,200 after customer 5.
- **Deposit:** take 50% up front via FPS or PayMe. This filters out people who aren't serious.
- **Niche:** pick one type of shop first, such as cafes, salons, florists or tutoring centres. You can reuse shot lists and templates, and your samples will look relevant to each next shop.
- **Timing:** it's October, so lead with "Christmas / Double 11 / CNY promo clip". Shops are planning those promotions now.

## Budget (HKD 500)

| Item | HKD |
|---|---|
| Phone tripod or clamp | ~120 |
| Clip-on mic | ~120 |
| Small LED light | ~120 |
| Printed one-page flyer with a QR code to a sample reel | ~60 |
| Buffer for transport and coffee | ~80 |

Edit in CapCut for free and use royalty-free music. Spend nothing on ads, because walking in the door converts better.

## Ideas, best first

1. **Walk in with a sample.** Pick 15 shops near campus or home. Make a free 10-second teaser from their own Instagram photos, then show it on your phone: "I made this for you, want the full version?" Their own shop is a stronger pitch than any portfolio.
2. **Free job for 2 shops, in exchange for a testimonial and permission to post the work.** Do this for only 2 shops. After that, everyone pays.
3. **Target shops with a weak Instagram.** Look for ones with good products, last post over 30 days ago and low views. They have a visible problem you can point to.
4. **Referral partners.** Ask shop-fitting contractors, freelance photographers and POS or delivery-platform reps to refer shops to you for 10–15% of the fee.
5. **Same-street clustering.** After one shop says yes, tell the neighbours: "I just did the cafe next door." This is the cheapest trust you'll get.
6. **Later, once you have about 5 customers:** offer a monthly plan of 4 clips for HKD 1,200.

## Weekly plan (10 hours a week)

**Week 1 (setup)**
- 3 hours: write the offer and a one-page flyer.
- 4 hours: make 2 sample clips with a friend's or family member's business.
- 1 hour: buy the kit.
- 2 hours: build a 40-shop target list in a spreadsheet.

**Weeks 2–3 (sell)**
- 6 hours: visit 20 shops in person, or send 30 WhatsApp or Instagram messages.
- 4 hours: make your first 1–2 deliveries, including the free ones.

**Weeks 4–8 (loop)**
- 4 hours a week: selling and follow-ups.
- 6 hours a week: producing 1–2 clips.
- After every delivery, ask for a 2-line review and one referral.

## Outreach message (adapt it, don't spam it)

> "Hi, I'm a uni student who makes short videos for local shops. I noticed [specific thing, e.g. your lemon tart] and made a 10s sample for you: [link]. I'm taking on 10 shops this month at HKD 800 for 3 clips. Want me to do one for yours?"

## Reality check

- Expect about 1 in 10 in-person pitches to close. Cold DMs often do 1–3%.
- To get 10 customers, plan on 60–100 real conversations. Track each one (shop, date, response, next step).
- **Kill test:** after 40 contacts, if you have fewer than 2 yeses, change the offer or the niche before contacting more shops.
- **Permissions:** get written permission, even a WhatsApp message, before filming staff or customers. Use only royalty-free music, because Instagram business accounts can't use most popular tracks.
