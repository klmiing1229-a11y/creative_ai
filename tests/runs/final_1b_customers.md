**Problem:** Get 10 paying customers for a short-video service for small shops, starting with no clients.
**Limits I used:** About 10 hours a week, HKD 500, you're a university student. I assumed you're in Hong Kong and can shoot with a phone.

## Obvious answers
What most AI replies would say. Listed on purpose; some may be exactly right.
1. **Free sample video for a shop, then ask them to pay for more.** Still the right call when the owner can't picture the result.
2. **Cold DMs on Instagram or Xiaohongshu with a portfolio link.** Still the right call when the shops are active online.
3. **Walk into nearby shops and show videos on your phone.** Still the right call when shops are within walking distance, since owners trust a face.
4. **Make 2–3 spec videos of local shops first as a portfolio.** Still the right call when you have nothing to show yet.
5. **Intro price, such as half price for the first video.** Still the right call when price is the main hesitation.
6. **Ask each customer for a testimonial and referrals.** Still the right call from customer 2 onward.
7. **Sell to people you know, such as classmates' family shops.** Still the right call because warm trust beats cold outreach.
8. **Monthly packages of 4 videos.** Still the right call once a customer is happy.

…and more of the same kind.

## Hidden assumptions
- The shop owner is the buyer.
- Each shop needs its own pitch, price and schedule.
- Proof is a file you make privately before the pitch.
- All of your 10 hours must cover finding, selling, filming and editing.
- What you sell is "a video", not a result the owner can see.

## Answers out of the box
### 1. Stretch: Picture Day
**Idea:** Pick one street or one mall floor and announce a fixed date. Offer a 15-second video shot in a 20-minute slot, one fixed price (try HKD 300), with only 6 slots. Hand-deliver a small flyer to about 15 shops. You shoot everything in one trip and edit in batch.
**Move used:** Borrow from a distant field. School photo day works by batching one visit, fixed slots and visible scarcity.
**Assumption broken:** Each shop needs its own pitch, price and schedule.
**Nearest obvious answer:** #3 (walking in), and **how this differs:** you sell a dated group slot at a set price, so neighbours see each other sign up, instead of negotiating one shop at a time.
**Cheapest test (under one week):** Print 15 flyers (about HKD 50–100), deliver them in one afternoon, and see whether 3 slots are booked within 5 days.
**Biggest risk:** Owners may refuse to pay in advance for something they haven't seen.

### 2. Leap: A supplier pays, not the shop
**Idea:** Find small local brands (food, skincare, accessories) that sell through other small shops. Offer to film their product inside each stockist's shop, billed to the brand at about HKD 150 per shop. The brand gets 10 pieces of content in real stores. Each shop gets a free video, which is your warm lead for a paid one later.
**Move used:** Make the problem disappear. You skip the 10 separate sales by finding one buyer who already has the 10 shops.
**Assumption broken:** The shop owner is the buyer.
**Nearest obvious answer:** #7 (selling through contacts), and **how this differs:** the buyer is a different party with a different budget, and the shops become a distribution channel instead of the customers.
**Cheapest test (under one week):** Message 10 local brands that list stockists on Instagram or their website. It costs HKD 0. You're looking for 2 replies asking for a quote.
**Biggest risk:** One brand paying for 10 videos may count as one customer rather than ten, and tiny brands may have no content budget.

### 3. Weird but testable: Become the customer
**Idea:** Spend your HKD 500 as a shopper. Buy something small (about HKD 100) at 4–5 shops, and ask while paying, "Can I film this for my portfolio?" Post each video publicly and tag the shop. A few days later, message the owner their view and save numbers and your price.
**Move used:** Invert. The seller becomes the buyer, so the owner is already positive and the proof is real reach instead of a file.
**Assumption broken:** Proof is a private file you make before the pitch.
**Nearest obvious answer:** #1 (free sample), and **how this differs:** the sample goes live on the shop's real audience, and the owner has just made a sale, so they see results rather than a promise.
**Cheapest test (under one week):** Do it at 2 shops for about HKD 200. If 1 owner asks the price, it works.
**Biggest risk:** A post that gets few views makes the pitch for that shop weaker than no post at all.
